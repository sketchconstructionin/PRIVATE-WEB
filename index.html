The Construction Phase Flow

A highly governed sequence ensuring structural strength and compliance at every step.

01

01 / Pre-Construction & Subsurface Analysis

We establish site benchmark coordinates, execute precise centerline layout markings, perform soil load bearing tests, and determine footing depths based on soil characteristics.

02

02 / Foundation & Substructure Casting

Excavation, laying PCC (Plain Cement Concrete) leveling course, binding footing reinforcement steel meshes, casting column starters, and pouring concrete footings followed by plinth beam binding.

03

03 / Column Binding & Vertical Casting

Erecting vertical column TMT reinforcement steel cages. We strictly check plumb-lines for vertical alignment before installing shuttering boxes, followed by concrete pouring and mechanical compaction vibration.

04

04 / Slab Formwork, Reinforcement & Casting

Erecting heavy-gauge steel shuttering/scaffolding, laying beam grids, binding double-layer slab reinforcement meshes, laying electrical PVC conduits, and casting the entire slab monolithically.

05

05 / Brick Masonry & Internal Plastering

Building high-integrity brick masonry walls using premium class-1 red bricks or AAC blocks. Checking wall alignment, laying plumbing and electrical wall conduits, and applying high-adhesion plastering.

06

06 / Compression QC & Civil Carcass Handover

Evaluating concrete cube compression test lab reports, checking walls for plumb and level tolerances, executing curing cycles (minimum 14-21 days), and delivering the certified structural shell.
